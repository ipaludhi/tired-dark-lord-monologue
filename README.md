Malachar, Lord of the Nine Ashen Kingdoms, has kept the same siege for three hundred years — and his last trap is to yield the throne to the hero. A 100-word villain monologue where the tiredness lives in the pauses and the dropped threads, never in a plain statement. Exactly 100 words, 515 characters.
